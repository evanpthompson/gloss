gloss — exposure test (run 3)

Asks the question a HUD actually poses: how long does a card have to be on screen before you can say what it was? The card appears for a fixed time, disappears, and then you answer. There is nothing to press while it is up, and nothing to wait out.

  • Second screen, fullscreen, the distance you actually sit.
  • A +, then a card, then a block of nonsense that wipes the afterimage. Look at the + and keep still.
  • Two questions per card: which term, and one specific detail. F / J or click. Guess when you did not catch it — a guess is data, a blank is not.
  • Some exposures are far too short to read. That is deliberate; do not take it as a failure.
  • Three practice cards first, not recorded.
