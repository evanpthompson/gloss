gloss — glance test

Measures whether a one-word card is taken in faster than the card that ships today, and whether moving the text helps or hurts. SPEC.md § 4b calls for this before any HUD code is written.

  • Put this on the second screen, fullscreen, at the distance you actually sit. Size is the variable being measured.
  • A card appears. Press SPACE the moment you have taken it in — not when you have finished reading every word.
  • Then two questions. Answer with F / J or click. Guess if you did not catch it; guessing is a real answer here.
  • Four practice trials first. They are not recorded.
